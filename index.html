FAQ

What is an NFC review card?

A card with a small NFC chip. When a phone is held near it, the phone opens a web page, in this case your Google review page.

How does a customer use it?

They tap their phone on the card, or scan the QR code with their camera. Your review page opens and they can write a review.

Will it work on every phone?

No. Most recent smartphones support NFC, but some don't, and some need NFC turned on in settings. That's why every kit also has a QR code.

What if NFC is unavailable?

The customer can scan the QR code instead. It works with any phone camera.

Can I add my business logo?

Yes. Send your logo on WhatsApp after you submit your order.

How do I share my Google review link?

Open your Google Business Profile, choose "Get more reviews" or "Share review form", and copy the link. Paste it in the order form.
